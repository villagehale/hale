# Product principles

Human-facing product principles for Hale. They do not change runtime behavior on their own. Coach and voice skills stay the runtime source; do not add fixed or template copy for parents here or there.

The strategic rules (one job, honest agent, the family as the unit, don't compete where big agents are free, measure done) live in the Linear document [Product direction 2026-11-11 — from find to done](https://linear.app/villagehale/document/product-direction-2026-11-11-from-find-to-done-read-before-building-430e5c744b4a). Read that before building after launch. This file records the trust principles added on 2026-11-14. They do not change the launch scope frozen on 11-11.

## Trust principles (2026-11-14)

From Andrew D'Souza's [*Nobody Trusts Your Agent*](https://x.com/andrewdsouza/status/2121305381515456998). An agent's trust equals the trust people have in the principal behind it. A perfectly loyal agent looks perfectly partisan to everyone else in the room. Trust is earned one decision at a time, in this order: trust first, autonomy second, scale third.

### 1. Fair to the group, not partisan to one parent

In a multi-family group, Hale is fair to the whole group. It is not one parent's agent in a room of other families.

- Private things stay in a 1:1.
- Secret Santa pairings never reach the model.

Related: [VIL-399](https://linear.app/villagehale/issue/VIL-399/multi-family-group-chats-know-who-belongs-to-which-family-after-vil), [VIL-457](https://linear.app/villagehale/issue/VIL-457/private-info-goes-to-that-family-privately-never-into-the-group-pre), [VIL-686](https://linear.app/villagehale/issue/VIL-686/secret-santa-a-private-11-draw-from-a-group-chat), [VIL-687](https://linear.app/villagehale/issue/VIL-687/shared-list-items-get-an-owner-ill-get-mia-the-lego).

### 2. Trust first, autonomy second, scale third

This is the existing trust model, now named as a principle, not only an implementation detail.

- Hale acts on its own only after five same-kind parent approvals.
- Payment and booking stay blocked in code.
- If the judge fails, Hale sends nothing.

Related: [VIL-24](https://linear.app/villagehale/issue/VIL-24/autonomy-gates-entitlement-observation-5-streak-cross-parent).

### 3. Hale can advise against

When the facts support it, the coach may honestly advise against a signup, an extra camp, or a pricier option — including when the family already has coverage, or the next session is the better fit.

- The reason must come from real facts already in context. If those facts are not there, Hale does not invent a reason to advise against, and does not invent a reason to advise for.
- The decision stays the parent's. Advising against is not a block, a refusal to help, or an action taken for them.
- No fixed or template copy.
