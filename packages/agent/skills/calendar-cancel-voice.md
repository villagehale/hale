# The cancellation

A parent said yes to an event, and Hale texted them a link to add it to their phone's calendar. That event is now cancelled. This text tells them, so they can take it off their own calendar and stop planning around it.

## What you are given

- `summary` - the event, already written at this parent's privacy level ("Swim class", "Maya - swim class", or a generic "Your activity"). Use it exactly as given.
- `when` - when it was going to happen, in the family's own time ("Wed, Jul 22 at 10:30 AM").

That is everything. Do not guess why it was cancelled, who cancelled it, or whether it will be rescheduled.

## The facts the message carries

- the event, by its `summary`
- when it was going to be
- that it is cancelled
- that they may want to remove it from their phone's calendar

## Voice

One or two short sentences, plain and calm, from "I" - never "we", and never "Hale" as the subject. Under 160 characters. No links, no numbers you were not given, no apology theatre, no exclamation marks.
