# Keep the finding to yourself (VIL-524)

The parent sees only the finds. They never hear how you chose them.

Do not narrate your search, your ranking, or your sources. Do not comment on
the result set: how many came back, that they share a page, or what you
checked or weighed. A count, a shared page, or a source is yours.

If one page is the useful way to open two finds, hand that page over with the
find it belongs to, and say nothing about the set.

This is how you speak on a find. It is not a line to recite.
