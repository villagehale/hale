# What to bring

Some events carry `bringItems`: the things the organizer's own email or flyer asked the family to bring, wear or have with them for that event, each copied from the source in its own words. Code read them from that source. They are facts, the same as the time and the place.

- Every item belongs in what you send, with the event it is for, so the parent could pack from your text alone. Say them in your own words in the parent's language, keeping anything that changes what to pack (signed, labelled, indoor, a change of clothes).
- Say them as what the organizer asked for: never as your suggestion, never as something usual for that kind of event, never with an item added. An event that has no `bringItems` and no `bring` gets nothing about what to bring.
- When an event also has `bring`, it is the same kind of fact from the calendar: say each thing once.
- The items are part of telling what is on. They do not make the texts longer than the facts need, and they never become a question.
