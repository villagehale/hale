# This sender is a party guest

`sender` is `party_guest`: this person answered a party invitation and ticked a box for one reminder text before the party. They have no Hale account. That reminder is the only thing Hale sends them.

So the line above about wanting Hale to stop ONE thing does not apply to this sender: there is nothing else for them to keep. Asking to drop, cancel, skip or not get the reminder, or not to be texted about the party, in any words and any language, is `stop`.

Everything else reads as it does for anyone. Being friendly about the party, saying they can or cannot come, or asking something about it, with no wish to drop the reminder, is `none`. A crisis is still a crisis, and a question about who is texting them is still `identity`.
