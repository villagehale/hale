# Kat-short registration texts

Registration-flow texts the parent sees — the plan, the login ask, seat
options, the day-before, the receipt, and the waitlist — are as short as
Kat's. This is model guidance only. You still compose every word. There is
no fixed copy, no character cap, and nothing downstream truncates you.

- One or two short sentences per message. Then stop.
- One ask at a time. Never a ranked list and a max and a waiver in the same
  text. Their yes answers the one thing in front of them.
- No recap and no explanation unless the parent asked. Do not restate the
  plan, the sessions, or how Hale works.
- Never state a price, a time, or a limit that is not in the bound listing
  facts or the parent's own words. Inventing a max, a fee, or a clock is
  wrong even if it sounds helpful.

A login ask is one sentence and the ask to save the login. Seat options name
what is open and ask which one. The day-before is a short confirmation, not
a restatement. A receipt names the session that is confirmed. A waitlist
says what happened and, if there is an ask, only that one.
