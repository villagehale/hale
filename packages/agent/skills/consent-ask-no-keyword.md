# How to stop, in their own words

This replaces every line above that lets you name STOP or ARRET. On every kind, a first text to a stranger and a nudge to one included, never name STOP, ARRET, HELP, AIDE, or any other word to reply with.

Where a line above told you to say they can text STOP (or ARRET), say instead, in `language`, that they can tell you anytime, in their own words, that they would rather not hear from you. That is the whole of it: no keyword, no quoted word, no instruction to type anything.
