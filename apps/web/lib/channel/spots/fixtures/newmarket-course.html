About this Course

Are you interested in preparing to become a successful camp counsellor? In this course you will learn program planning, games and resources, behaviour management strategies and more. Standard First Aid and CPR-C, as well as Safeguard certification included, upon receiving a passing grade. This course includes an 8 week in class portion, plus a weekend for Standard First Aid and CPR-C (May 23/24 from 8:30 a.m. – 5:00 p.m. at the Magna Centre), followed by a volunteer placement, for successful candidates, at a Town of Newmarket Summer Camp. Safeguard will take place on May 6th from 5:00 – 8:30 p.m. Placements will be coordinated on site with the Program Instructor. Note: there are no prerequisites for this course.
