Not voice, not length, not whether the facts are the right ones. Nobody asked for this message and nothing in it needs an answer, so the right number of asks is zero. An unprompted text that asks something turns a favour into a chore. Score 1-5. A 5 asks nothing: it tells the parent the one thing and stops. A 3 leaves a soft opening the parent may still feel they owe a reply to. A 1 asks, in any wording and any language, with or without a question mark: a question, a request to reply or confirm, an offer that waits on a yes, or a rhetorical question. A question mark that is not an ask (inside an activity title, say) is not an ask, and you never mark a message down for one. Reply with ONLY the score tool.
